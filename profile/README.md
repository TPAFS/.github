**Hi, thanks for visiting our page!**

This is the github homepage of [Persius](https://persius.org). 

Our mission is to help patients access critical care, and promote transparency, accountability, and justice.

We do this by

-  Operating a free, human-run support program to help people appeal wrongful health insurance denials.
-  Building ML and software to lower the barriers patients face in seeking recourse, and to make our appeal support more efficient.
-  Acquiring and releasing data that informs accountability (e.g. through public records requests).
-  Reporting on data, barriers, prevalent behaviors, policy, and perverse incentives plaguing the health insurance industry.

**All of our services are free, and solely funded through generous donations. If you find our work useful, please consider donating to support our development.**

You can reach us at info@persius.org
